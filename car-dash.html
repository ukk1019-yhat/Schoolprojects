How to play: Drive your car along the 4000 metre highway through 10 levels (400 m each). Dodge the traffic cars, cones and barriers using the arrow keys / WASD or by dragging your finger! Don’t crash — hit 3 obstacles and you are out. Catch a gold star to earn a new life. Each level makes the road faster and busier. Finish all 4000 m to win!
