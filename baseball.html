Two players. One big homerun battle!

⚾ HOW TO PLAY

  • P1 bats first. P2 pitches and fields!
  • Pitcher: press the big button to throw the ball.
  • Batter: press SWING when the ball comes close!
  • Perfect swing = HOME RUN! +5 🎉
  • Good swing = Safe hit +2 ⚾
  • Miss or swing too early = STRIKE 💢
  • 3 strikes = you are OUT, switch players!
  • Fielder: tap CATCH to catch the homerun and OUT the batter!
  • Most points wins the game! 🏆
