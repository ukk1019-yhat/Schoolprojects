How to Play

Goal

Ride to the finish line. Grab glowing spirit orbs, rescue little lost spirits, and collect stars for big points.

Keyboard

A / ← brake & lean back
D / → accelerate & lean forward
Space jump & spin in the air
Shift boost
P pause   R restart

Touch

Big buttons on screen: arrows to move, JUMP to leap and do tricks, BOOST to go faster.

Power-ups

Spirit Shield blocks one bump.
Super Boost = super speed.
Orb Magnet pulls in orbs.
Super Jump = higher leaps.

Tricks

Hold a direction while airborne to spin. Land cleanly for bonus points & cheers!

Friendly fun

No scary stuff. Bats and floaters just poof away into sparkles. Wipe-outs only cost a little speed.
