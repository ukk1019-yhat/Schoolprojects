👻 GHOST SCARE

Can you scare everyone in the house?

👻 HOW TO PLAY

  • 👻 Move your ghost around the house.
  • 🔎 Find humans.
  • 😱 Get close enough to scare them with SPACE.
  • 🪙 Earn coins for every scared human.
  • 🧠 Brave humans need stronger scare combinations (press 1/2/3, fire with E).
  • 💡 Use special abilities: Pop, Lights Out & Super Scare!
  • ⚡ Grab power-ups hiding around the house.
  • 🏆 Scare EVERY human before the timer ends!
